Shardfall: The Sundering Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

F7 Developer operates Shardfall: The Sundering ("Shardfall"). This policy explains what information Shardfall handles when you use optional online features, Google sign-in, and Google Play purchases.

Information we handle

Signing in is optional. If you choose Google sign-in, Shardfall and its backend receive basic account information Google makes available for sign-in, such as your email address, display name, and a Google account identifier. We use it to create and secure your Shardfall account and associate your cloud save and paid entitlements with it.

With sign-in, Shardfall can store a cloud save snapshot containing game progress, collection, decks, settings, achievements, quests, and in-game currency balances. The backend also stores Google Play product IDs, purchase tokens, order IDs, Gold amounts, verification results, and refund or chargeback state. This delivers purchased Gold, restores it after reinstall, prevents duplicate delivery, and removes an entitlement when Google Play reports a voided purchase.

Google Play processes payment. Shardfall does not receive or store your credit-card number, bank details, or Google Play payment credentials.

Use and sharing

We use this information to provide sign-in and cloud save, restore progress and verified Gold, verify purchases, handle refunds, protect the purchase ledger, and respond to support. Shardfall has no advertising and does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Google provides sign-in and billing, and Supabase provides authentication, database, and server-side functions.

Local storage

Shardfall can run offline. Local data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall. A signed-in copy of the selected save and verified entitlements may remain in the cloud until deletion is requested.

Retention and deletion

To request deletion of your Shardfall account and associated cloud save data, email fajar.mreza@gmail.com from the account you used to sign in with the subject “Shardfall account deletion”. We delete data we control except information retained for security, fraud prevention, legal, tax, or payment-record purposes.

Children and security

Shardfall is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. We use access controls and authenticated backend requests, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version remains available at this public URL with a new update date.
