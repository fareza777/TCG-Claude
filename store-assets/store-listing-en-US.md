## Full description

VAEL HAS FALLEN. THE WORLD HAS SHATTERED. BUILD THE DECK THAT SURVIVES.

Shardfall: The Sundering is a dark fantasy trading card game set in Aethyr, a world split by the fall of the star Vael. Collect cards, shape a 40-card deck, and master tactical battles where every point of Aether matters.

DUEL OTHER PLAYERS
Enter real-time online PvP, choose your battle deck, and test your strategy against another player. Read the board, assign blockers, time your responses, and turn a single card into a decisive play. Google sign-in and an internet connection are required for PvP.

EXPLORE A STORY-RICH CAMPAIGN
Follow Chapter I: The Waking Grove and discover what survived the Sundering. The campaign, tutorial, Skirmish AI battles, deck building, and collection management can be played without signing in.

MASTER FIVE DOMINIONS
• Verdance — growth and mighty Units
• Pyre — fast attacks and direct damage
• Tide — insight, tempo, and control
• Dawn — protection, healing, and unity
• Gloom — sacrifice, decay, and revival

BUILD YOUR COLLECTION
• Collect 192 cards from the first set
• Open Shard Packs and discover new strategies
• Craft cards and refine multiple 40-card decks
• Learn original keywords including Soar, Rush, Aegis, Rampage, and Awaken

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
• Challenge tactical AI opponents in Skirmish
• Draft a temporary deck and face the Arena gauntlet
• Complete quests and achievements
• Earn Gold through play or purchase optional Gold packs
• Remove advertising permanently with an optional one-time purchase

BUILT FOR MOBILE CARD BATTLES
Shardfall combines the decisions of a collectible card game with focused mobile sessions, atmospheric fantasy art, and an original fractured world. Start as a guest for single-player play, then link your Google account when you are ready for PvP, cloud-backed progress, and verified purchases.

The star is broken. The Dominions are rising. Choose what Aethyr becomes next.
